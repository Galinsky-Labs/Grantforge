Federal funding prerequisites

To receive any federal grant the applicant must hold an active SAM.gov registration (free, issues the UEI) and, to apply through Grants.gov, an account with the Authorized Organization Representative role. Both take days to weeks; start them before the first federal deadline. Individuals can register in SAM.gov as a sole proprietor, but most federal opportunities limit eligibility to organisations; the matcher filters on the entity type above so you are not shown grants you cannot win.
